A paper by _Subaru Shibai_ and _Kiwamu Izumi_ is now published in JOSA A. Congraturations!

This paper proposes a compact optical system that reproduces long-distance laser beam propagation for ground testing of intersatellite optical links, shows that at least three lenses are needed, and numerically examines the trade-off between compactness and manufacturing precision.
